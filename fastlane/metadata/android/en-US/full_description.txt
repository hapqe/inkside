Inkside is a notebook for studying and coding. Write on your PDFs with a stylus, circle the part you don't understand, and ask. The AI agent sees your marks.

<b>Write on anything</b>
• Mark up lecture slides, textbooks and papers with pen, highlighter and typed text
• Palm rejection, pen buttons, snap-to-shape and effect brushes
• Typeset maths with LaTeX next to your handwriting
• Reorder pages, search your PDFs and handwriting, export a normal PDF

<b>Ask the AI about the page</b>
• Circle or box something, then ask in the chat
• A tutor that asks before it tells
• Interactive visualizations, dictation and a code editor with run

<b>Everything in sync</b>
• Documents live on your device and work fully offline
• Connect a computer once and your tablet and phone share the same documents, ink and chats
• No Inkside account and no cloud service

The agent features are optional. They run through the Inkside host, a small program on your own computer (see the project page), using your own Claude subscription or API key. Without it, the notebook still works fully.

Handwriting search uses Google ML Kit digital ink (on-device, proprietary).
