## What it does

- Pen colours and three quick sizes.
- Effect brushes: rainbow, glow, sparkle, calligraphy and spray.
- Stylus settings: palm rejection, snap, pen outline and pen buttons.
- Pick any of 40+ colour themes, light or dark.
